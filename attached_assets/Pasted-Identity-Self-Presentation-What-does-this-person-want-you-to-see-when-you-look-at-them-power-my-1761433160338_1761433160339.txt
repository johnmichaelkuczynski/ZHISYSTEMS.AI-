Inner Life & Psychology
10. Does this person look comfortable in their own skin, or self-conscious?
11. Do they seem used to being observed, or visibly awkward?
12. Is there alertness or dulled exhaustion in their gaze?
13. Do they appear inward-facing (thinking), outward-facing (performing), or disconnected?
14. Do they look like someone who notices everything? Or someone living inside their head?

Values & Priorities
15. What kind of order do they impose on their environment (discipline vs chaos vs deliberate neglect)?
16. Does what they’re wearing express rebellion, austerity, elegance, apathy, wealth, or nothing at all?
17. Is there evidence of care (grooming, posture, lighting) or of fatigue and disregard?
18. Does their environment suggest art, intellect, survival, religion, vanity, or emptiness?

Social Positioning
19. What type of person would they willingly spend time with?
20. Do they give off cues of class — upwardly mobile, bohemian, military, academic, working-class, etc.?
21. Do they seem like a leader? Outsider? Observer?
22. Are they signaling toughness, gentleness, cynicism, idealism, eccentricity?

Competence & Authenticity
23. If they’re next to a piano — do their hands belong to someone who practices, or someone who poses?
24. If wearing sunglasses indoors — is it self-mockery, insecurity, intimidation, or wanting to disappear?
25. The all-black — is it laziness? discipline? aesthetic minimalism? mourning? rock-star cosplay?
26. Is the posture of someone trained (military, athletic) or someone physically neglected?

Intellect & Disposition
27. Does the face show calculation, openness, suspicion, imagination, obsession?
28. Is this someone capable of deep attention — or perpetual distraction?
29. Is the intelligence in their eyes cold-analytic, dreamy-creative, mechanical, mystical, or absent?
30. If they had to choose between being liked, respected, feared, or left alone — which do they choose?
