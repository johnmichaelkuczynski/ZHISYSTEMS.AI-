# 知 ZHI SYSTEMS

**High-Performance AI Tools, Living Books, Living Courses, and an Interdisciplinary Research Journal -- in One Platform**

## 🧩 Overview

Zhi Systems is a unified showcase and access layer for a working library of AI-powered intellectual tools. From a single front door, users reach 28+ production applications, an alphabetical catalog of Living Books, a growing roster of fully-built Living Courses, a 100+ issue academic journal with on-demand AI analysis, and a podcast library of AI-generated episodes drawn from that journal. Every linked artifact is a real, deployed product -- not a demo, not a mockup, not a coming-soon page.

Unlike a generic portfolio site or an AI-tool directory, Zhi Systems is built around a strict operating principle: every output is rigorous, every claim is explicit, and nothing is mocked. The journal renders publication-quality long-form essays with built-in rewrite, study guide, test, podcast, cognitive-map, deep-dive, summary, and TTS audio generation. The course catalog points to fully working LMS chassis with AI tutors, originality checks, sequential gating, and end-to-end diagnostics. The applications page links out to live products covering writing, education, intelligence and psychology, multimedia, audio, and utilities.

## 👥 Who It's For

- **Academics, graduate students, and researchers** -- need a single source for high-rigor AI tools (intelligence scoring, axiomatization, proof checking, cognitive mapping) plus an interdisciplinary journal covering philosophy, mathematical philosophy, evolutionary game theory, epistemology, and metaphysics
- **Instructors, course operators, and curriculum designers** -- need turn-key Living Courses (English Composition, Algebra, U.S. History I/II, Western Civ, American Government, Public Speaking, Data Analytics, Business 101, and more) with AI-graded submissions, GPTZero originality checks, and one-click diagnostics
- **Writers, philosophers, and theorists** -- need on-demand AI rewrite, cognitive analysis, formal axiomatization, and objection-proof synthesis applied to their own drafts
- **Students and self-directed learners** -- need free public access to a multi-volume research journal, an AI podcast library, and dozens of free-to-use applications

- **Anyone** -- who wants a single trusted home for serious AI tools instead of a directory of half-finished startups

## ⚙️ Core Capabilities

- **Zhi Systems Journal** -- A 100+ issue, 75+ volume academic journal with Roman-numeral volume/issue numbering, markdown long-form articles, and an admin interface for publication. Covers philosophy, mathematical philosophy, evolutionary game theory, epistemology, metaphysics, political economy, and systematic reinterpretations of classical theories.

- **8 AI Analysis Functions Per Article** -- Every published issue ships with one-click Rewrite, Study Guide, Test, Podcast Script, Visual Cognitive Map, Deep Dive, Summary, and TTS audio generation. All outputs are downloadable in structured formats.

- **OpenAI TTS-1 Audio Generation** -- Any article body can be turned into a downloadable spoken audio file in seconds; the same pipeline powers the standalone Podcast Library.

- **Podcast Library** -- 14 episodes of AI-generated audio aligned with the journal's research arcs, browsable and playable directly in the browser.

- **Living Books Catalog** -- An alphabetically-sorted catalog of long-form Living Books, with clear attribution for John-Michael Kuczynski on 13 specific titles.

- **Living Courses Catalog** -- 20+ fully-built online course shells (English Composition 101/102, College Algebra, Algebra 2, Prealgebra, Developmental Math, Statistics, Data Analytics, Business 101, American Government, Western Civilization, Public Speaking, U.S. History I/II, Systems Science, Medical Terminology, Philosophy 101, and more), each linking to its own production deployment with AI tutors, originality verification, and sequential gating.

- **28+ Core Applications** -- Organized into six categories (Writing & Books, Education & Learning, Intelligence & Psychology, Visual & Multimedia, Audio & Interactive Media, Utility & Conversion), every entry links out to a live product, not a placeholder.

- **Video Tutorial System** -- Apps with `videoUrl` show a prominent "📹 Tutorial" button that opens an in-page video player. Nine tutorials currently shipped; the schema lets new ones light up the moment a video file is added.

- **Persistent PostgreSQL Storage** -- Journal issues, admin state, and dynamic content live in a real Neon-hosted Postgres database via Drizzle ORM. No in-memory shims; data survives every restart and deploy.

- **Password-Gated Admin Surface** -- The journal admin and podcast admin views are gated behind a shared password so the public reader experience stays clean while editorial work happens in the same app.

- **Installable PWA Across All Platforms** -- Ships with a web app manifest, Apple touch icons, Microsoft tile icons, and a Safari pinned-tab icon. The site can be added to the home screen as a standalone 知 app on iPhone, iPad, Android, macOS Dock, and the Windows Start menu.

- **SEO + Open Graph Out of the Box** -- Every page has a unique descriptive title, meta description, and Open Graph tags so journal issues, courses, and applications share cleanly to social and chat.

- **Responsive, Mobile-First UI** -- React + TypeScript + Vite, shadcn/ui on Radix UI primitives, Tailwind CSS with CSS variables, Lucide icons, minimal animation, direct functionality.

- **Single Source of Truth for Content** -- Static book and application data is hardcoded in typed TypeScript files; dynamic journal/podcast content lives in Postgres. Each piece of content has exactly one home.

## 🚀 What Makes It Different

- **Every link is a real product** -- Every course URL, every application URL, and every Living Book entry points to a deployed, working artifact. There are no "coming soon" cards, no parked domains, no mocked previews.

- **The journal is publication-grade, not a blog** -- Volume/issue numbering, Roman numerals, multi-issue volume series, named contributing authors, and full markdown rendering. Volume XLVII alone runs to 11 issues of systematic reinterpretations of Leibniz, Rawls, James, Berkeley, Plato, Hegel, Marx, Spinoza, and Kant.

- **AI is a function of the content, not the product** -- The 8 analysis tools (rewrite, study guide, test, podcast, cognitive map, deep dive, summary, TTS) act on the journal's own essays. The AI exists to interrogate the writing, not to produce it.

- **Courses are entire applications, not landing pages** -- Every Living Course URL is a separately-deployed LMS chassis with its own database, AI tutor, originality stack, sequential gating, and one-click diagnostics. Zhi Systems is the catalog; each course is the cathedral.

- **Identical course chassis across the catalog** -- U.S. History I, U.S. History II, Western Civilization, Public Speaking, American Government, English Composition, Algebra, and the rest share the same structural conventions: 14-module curriculum, modeled responses, GPTZero on every submission, server-enforced module order, end-to-end walk diagnostic.

- **One admin surface, many surfaces of consumption** -- Journal issues post once and are immediately available to read, to feed the 8 AI tools, to power the podcast library, and to anchor TTS audio downloads. There is no second copy to keep in sync.

- **No silent fallbacks, no fake data** -- If a dependency is unavailable, the system says so. If a video tutorial doesn't exist yet, no button is rendered. If an AI call fails, the user sees the failure -- never a hallucinated success.

- **Designed for the home screen, not just the browser tab** -- Installable as a standalone app on iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows with a real square 知 icon, theme color, and standalone display mode. Zhi Systems looks and behaves like a native app the moment it's installed.

- **A single coherent identity** -- The 知 mark (zhī -- knowledge, wisdom) appears on the logo, the favicon, the installed-app icon, the Start-menu tile, the Dock icon, and the home-screen bookmark. One brand, one symbol, one site, one source of truth.
